3D Interactive Motion Rehab • Inspired by iTraining

Safe, gamified 3D rehabilitation for hip replacement recovery.

Use your phone, tablet, or computer camera for real-time joint motion tracking. Hit 3D coin boxes to build Range of Motion, practice seated lower-garment dressing with a 3D pants guide, and stay protected with automatic 90° hip precaution alerts.

1. 3D ROM Coin Game

Select Hip, Knee, or Ankle. Set your target range and move your limb to hit the 3D coin box for spinning coin animations and rewarding sound feedback.

👖

2. 3D ADL Pants Dressing

Seated dressing practice tracking both hands from Toes → Ankle → Knee → Hip, then reversing to take off, synced with a real-time 3D animated pants model.

🛡️

3. 90° Hip Precaution Guard

Real-time posture safety monitor. Prompts you as hip flexion nears 90° and triggers an immediate pop-up warning and sound alert if it exceeds 90°.

⚠ Post-Hip Replacement Precaution Reminders

  • Do NOT bend your operated hip past 90° (avoid low chairs or leaning too far forward).
  • Do NOT cross your operated leg over the midline of your body.
  • Do NOT twist your operated leg inward or outward.
  • Always follow the specific instructions from your doctor or occupational therapist.
Privacy & Safety Notice: All camera motion tracking runs locally inside your browser — no video is recorded or uploaded. This app is an interactive rehabilitation aid and does not replace clinical assessment. Stop immediately if you feel pain or dizziness.
